# Handoff: redesign the Beijing trip site around three.js / WebGL

Written 2026-11-21. Trip starts 7 Dec 2026 (16 days away). Build, verify, and publish.

## Goal

The owner wants the site to feel special, with three.js / WebGL as the centre of the visual design rather than a snow overlay. Keep it a fast, usable itinerary: they will open it dozens of times a day, one-handed, in the cold.

## What exists (read these first)

- Live: `https://captainjry.github.io/bj26-x7k2/` (public repo `captainjry/bj26-x7k2`, deploys on every push to `main` via `.github/workflows/pages.yml`, publishes the `site/` folder).
- Code: `README.md` (decisions, `trip.json` schema, update steps) → `site/index.html`, `site/app.js` (270 lines, screens), `site/logic.js` (date/now/next/hotel/map-URL logic, keep), `site/snow.js` (current three.js scene, 92 lines), `site/style.css` (355 lines), `site/sw.js` (offline cache, `APP_VERSION` currently '3').
- Data: `site/trip.json` is generated by `build.py` from the owner's sheet — never hand-edit it. Places carry `photo`, days carry `cover` (Wikimedia photos in `site/img/`, credited on `#credits`).
- three.js r170 is vendored at `site/vendor/three.module.min.js`. Fonts: Prompt (headings) + Noto Sans Thai (body), woff2 in `site/fonts/`, @font-face at the top of `style.css`.

## Users and hard constraints

- Couple from Thailand. UI labels English; their sheet text is Thai/English mix; they **cannot read Chinese**. Chinese appears only on the "Show to driver" card.
- Phones: her **iPhone 18 Pro** (Safari, installed to home screen), his **Samsung S24+** (Chrome). Gloves, -10 to -20 °C, bright snow glare, battery drains fast in cold.
- Offline and China: no CDN or font/API requests at runtime. Every asset is vendored under `site/` and precached in `site/sw.js`. Keep total precache under ~5 MB (now ~2.8 MB).
- Keep unchanged in behaviour: `logic.js` functions and `site/test_logic.mjs`, the driver card (black on white, huge Chinese, tap anywhere to close), Map buttons (Apple Maps on iOS, Amap on Android), `?today=YYYY-MM-DD&time=HH:MM` test params, Credits screen, install help, update bar, `noindex`.
- WebGL rules: render loop pauses when the page is hidden or the canvas is off screen; cap devicePixelRatio at 2; target 30 fps or less when idle; full static fallback (CSS) under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` and when WebGL fails — the itinerary must work identically without it. Text never sits on moving content without a solid scrim (contrast ≥ 4.5:1).
- Usable first: itinerary content visible on first paint; WebGL fades in after. Tap targets ≥ 44 px.

## Direction (yours to judge)

Use the `ui-ux-pro-max:ui-ux-pro-max` skill (Skill tool) for the design system and its pre-delivery checklist; the owner asked for it. Ideas to weigh, not orders:
- A 3D route/map scene: Beijing → Chongli → Beijing as a stylised terrain/line the camera flies along when you switch days.
- Day covers as WebGL planes with depth/parallax, light snowfall and soft fog driven by scroll.
- Scroll-linked transitions between days; a winter night sky on the countdown screen.
Pick one coherent concept, not a collection of effects. Write the concept in 3–5 lines at the top of your final report and into the README "Decisions" section.

## How to work

- You run in your own git worktree of this repo. Another agent is writing `FEATURES-PROPOSAL.md` in the main checkout at the same time; do not touch that file.
- Test locally: `python -m http.server 8765` from `site/`, headless Chrome (`C:/Program Files/Google/Chrome/Application/chrome.exe`, use DevTools-protocol phone emulation at 390×844 and 375×667, light and dark, `?today=` before/during/after trip). Read your screenshots and fix what looks wrong. Check the console is clean and there is no horizontal scroll. Kill the server after (`pkill` is missing on this Windows machine; use PowerShell `Stop-Process`).
- Keep checks passing: `python -I test_build.py`, `node site/test_logic.mjs`. Bump `APP_VERSION` in `site/sw.js` and precache every new file.
- Temp files go in your scratchpad, and are deleted when done.

## Publish (owner's standing rule: publish without asking, they review the live site)

1. Commit by explicit paths (never `git add -A`), message format `type(scope): description`, e.g. `feat(site): webgl route scene`. Git identity is already set in this repo's config (GitHub noreply); do not change it.
2. Merge your branch into `main`, push `main`. Then confirm `gh run list -L 1` succeeds and `https://captainjry.github.io/bj26-x7k2/sw.js` serves the new `APP_VERSION`.
3. If the deploy fails, fix and push again; never force-push.

## Done when

The new design is live, all checks pass, screenshots at both phone sizes look right in light and dark, the fallback without WebGL works, and the README Decisions section describes the concept. Report: the concept, live URL, precache size, test results, anything left rough.
